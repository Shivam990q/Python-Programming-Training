⏱️ Afternoon 3-Hour Master Timeline

Carefully timed breakdown designed to maximize student engagement, concept retention, and coding speed.

00 – 35 MIN
List Comprehensions

Syntax anatomy `[expr for x in it if cond]`. CPython bytecode vs loops. Filtering vs ternary value substitution.

35 – 60 MIN
Nested & Built-ins

2D matrix flattening, matrix transposition. Companion built-ins: `sorted(key=)`, `enumerate(start=1)`, `zip(*matrix)` unzip.

60 – 70 MIN
Interactive Visual Labs

Interactive pipeline simulation: step-by-step stream filtering and accumulator visualization in the browser.

70 – 90 MIN
Strings & Memory Model

Literals, raw strings `r"..."`, escape sequences (`\n`, `\t`, `\r`, `\b`). CPython immutability & pointer reassignment.

90 – 115 MIN
Indexing & Slicing

Dual-directional indexing (0 to $N-1$ & $-1$ to $-N$). Slicing `s[start:stop:step]`, step reversals, out-of-range bounds safety.

115 – 155 MIN
23 String Methods & F-Strings

18 core methods + 5 advanced methods (`center`, `zfill`, `partition`, `isalnum`, `isspace`). F-strings alignment & formatting.

155 – 180 MIN
Colab & MCQ Assessment

Hands-on coding challenges in Google Colab followed by 35-question timed placement test in the assessment engine.
